# Watering Schedule

## Current rules

- Check moisture every 30 minutes between 06:00 and 20:00.
- Water a bed when it drops below 35% and it has not been watered in the last 4 hours.
- Run the pump for 20 seconds per bed, then re-check after 15 minutes.
