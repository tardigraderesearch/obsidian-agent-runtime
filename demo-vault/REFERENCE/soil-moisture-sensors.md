# Soil Moisture Sensors

## Resistive

Two exposed electrodes; measures conductivity. Cheap, but the electrodes corrode
(electrolysis), so readings drift within weeks. Fine for short experiments.

## Capacitive

Electrodes are sealed under a coating; measures the soil's dielectric permittivity.
No corrosion, so readings stay stable for seasons. Needs per-probe calibration
(dry and wet reference readings) because boards vary.

## Practical tips

- Insert to the same depth every time; depth changes the reading.
- Keep the electronics end above the soil line and out of the rain.
- Average several samples; single readings are noisy.
